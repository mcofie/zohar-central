Good to know

Questions, answered.

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings, 9–12 months gives you the widest choice of dates and vendors. Smaller celebrations can often come together in 8–12 weeks — if your date is sooner, ask anyway.

Do you plan events in both Ghana and Canada?

Yes. We plan celebrations in Accra and across Canada, and we love bridging the two — including traditional engagements in Ghana for couples who live abroad.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. It’s a relaxed, no-obligation conversation, in person or online, about your vision, guest list and budget. Book a time through our calendar or send an enquiry.

Can we book just décor hire or just coordination?

Absolutely. Every service can be booked on its own, and you can add more support later if you need it.

How do payments work?

A deposit secures your date, and the balance is spread across scheduled payments during planning. Your proposal sets out the exact amounts and dates before you commit.

Can you work with our budget?

We’ll always be honest about what your budget can achieve and where it will make the biggest impact — and we’ll suggest smart ways to stretch it.
